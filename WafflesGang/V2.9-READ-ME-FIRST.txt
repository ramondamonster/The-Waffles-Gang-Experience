Waffles Gang v2.9.0 — quick start

1. Close the game and replace WafflesGang with this folder.
2. Reload and tap B. It should open Pantry; tap B again to close.
3. Use /wg appearance. Choose Cake, Pie, Pizza or Samurai and a matching
   colorway. Switch away and back to verify that the choice was retained.
4. Use /wg gear. The addon should display your class, plus AUTO and only
   your three talent trees. AUTO attempts to detect the invested tree.
5. Use /wg guild. Verify the roster is legible and Invite to Party works
   when server permission and party leadership allow it.
6. Verify cooldown numbers, action bars, sorting and item use after reload.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- Run /console scriptErrors 1 then /reload and copy the error text.
- /wg bags opens the Pantry even if another addon claims B.
- /wg layout includes the B key override toggle.
- /wg recover disables Waffle replacements to restore the default UI.

Testing note: offline syntax and simulated startup/interaction tests passed;
real Forever client testing remains required.
