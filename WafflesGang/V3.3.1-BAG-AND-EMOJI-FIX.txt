WAFFLES GANG 3.3.1 | BAG SHORTCUT + DISCORD EMOJI HOTFIX

Built on Rain's v3.0, plus our v3.1/v3.2/v3.3 additions.
No changes to Rain's art, appearance, or HUD positions.

WHAT CHANGED
- Prevents rapid double delivery of a B press (key click + native bag hook)
  from immediately closing the Pantry.
- Reasserts Pantry B binding once shortly after entering the world, useful
  when other addons initialize their bindings after Waffles Gang.
- /wg bindbags explicitly reenables the B shortcut and refreshes the binding.
- /wg diag shows the active B binding and how many Waffles Gang key clicks were received.
- Strips unsupported emoji glyphs from event display, even if the existing
  EventFeed.lua was created by the older companion.

IN-GAME QUICK TEST
1. /wg bags should open/close the Pantry.
2. Outside chat, press B. Type /wg diag to check key press count.
3. If B is captured by Baganator or another bag addon, disable only its bag
   replacement or temporarily disable that addon, then /reload and /wg bindbags.
4. The Layout page also has USE B KEY FOR WAFFLE PANTRY.

INSTALL
Fully exit the game. Back up WafflesGang and WTF. Replace the entire
AddOns/WafflesGang directory; do not merge over the old folder.
After installing, re-run the companion sync (or preserve your existing
EventFeed.lua) because the bundled feed is an empty placeholder.

This patch is offline-tested, NOT verified inside the Forever client.
