Waffles Gang v3.9.2 | Modern HUD and Quest Tracker combined preview

Modern changes: floating framed-light action icons and adjustable spacing,
slim translucent XP bar and separate XP label, portrait-free lighter unit
frames, low-profile threat meter, and lightly translucent tracker.
Waffle Studio > UI Styles > Modern HUD Controls exposes icon size, spacing,
XP width, opacity and label. Settings remain independent of Rain themes.

Shared tracker: campaign quests pinned to top ONLY when the client flags
them (C_CampaignInfo.IsCampaignQuest). Complete quests can be grouped at
the bottom. Both sorting options in Waffle Studio > Quests & Maps.

Classic and all original theme files unchanged. Solo/Group/Raid HUD positions
are preserved. Avoid style changes in combat.

This package is a preview; actual WoW Forever client testing is required.
Back up your WafflesGang folder and WTF, exit WoW, then replace folder
completely. Do not merge installations.
